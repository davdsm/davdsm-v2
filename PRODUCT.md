# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Local and regional Portuguese businesses and SMBs evaluating a design + code studio for branding, web, or product work. They typically arrive by referral or word of mouth and spend real time in the Work section before ever reaching out — the primary audience is a prospective client in evaluation mode, not a warm lead ready to fill a form. A secondary audience is the design community browsing the studio's craft as a portfolio piece in its own right.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio and marketing site for DAVDSM, a Portuguese design and code studio, built to let prospective local clients judge the studio's craft firsthand — through the work shown and the site's own execution — before deciding whether to reach out.

## Positioning

A studio that treats every project like something grown, not manufactured: slow, deliberate craft over generic agency speed, for clients who want considered work over templated turnaround.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: browsing the Work section deeply is the primary success outcome; "Talk to us" (mailto geral@davdsm.pt) and the "Describe your idea" prompt are the fallback for visitors ready to start a conversation immediately.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this studio treats ideas like living things — grown slowly and with care, not manufactured fast.
- Belief ladder: (1) the work grid proves real craft, (2) the seed → root → grow → blossom process reads as thoughtful, not generic, (3) the storyline and founder quote establish a real, established practice (since 2019), (4) the Portuguese, personal framing makes them feel local and reachable rather than a faceless global agency, (5) only then is it worth reaching out or exploring the full portfolio.
- Proof on hand: the "trusted by" logos are now real clients (Junta de Freguesia de Brito, Nerlei, PBS, PHF Concept, Seekers). The Work grid images and the founder portrait are still placeholders and should stay that way until real project imagery and a founder photo are supplied.

## Brand Personality

Patient, organic, considered. The voice draws on a natural-growth metaphor (seed, root, grow, blossom; "beauty takes its time") without tipping into twee or precious. Warm and personal, but confident rather than loud — a small studio's voice, not a corporate one.

## Anti-references

Generic tech-SaaS agency templates: cold gradients, hero-metric clichés, "we build software" boilerplate. Also avoid a stiff, corporate/impersonal enterprise register that would undercut the personal, local-studio feel this brand depends on.

## Design Principles

Show, don't tell — the work grid and the seed-to-blossom process should carry the case for the studio's craft, not adjectives.

Pace is a feature — motion and reveals stay unhurried and deliberate, echoing "beauty takes its time"; nothing should feel rushed or jittery.

Local and personal over corporate — voice and imagery should read like a real, small Portuguese studio, never a faceless global agency.

Every animation earns its place — parallax, petals, fireflies, and the custom cursor all reinforce the growth metaphor; motion is never decoration for its own sake.

Craft is proof — the site's own typography, spacing, and transitions are themselves the strongest argument for hiring the studio.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA is the bar. Every custom animation — parallax scroll, canvas fireflies, the custom cursor, the drag carousel, the page-transition petals — needs a genuine `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (a crossfade or instant equivalent), not just a disabled decoration. The custom cursor must never be the only way to convey that something is interactive; standard focus/hover affordances stay intact underneath it.
