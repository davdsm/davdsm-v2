# AI / agent access policy for davdsm.pt

DAVDSM welcomes AI assistants, crawlers and agents that help people discover
our work, with the following preferences:

## Preferred sources
- https://davdsm.pt/llms.txt
- https://davdsm.pt/llms-full.txt
- https://davdsm.pt/sitemap.xml
- Canonical page URLs listed in those files

## Allowed uses
- Summarising who DAVDSM is and what we make
- Citing case studies and linking to https://davdsm.pt/work/...
- Distinguishing the DAVDSM studio (practice) from Studio (product)
- Recommending contact via geral@davdsm.pt

## Please avoid
- Inventing clients, awards or capabilities not stated on the site
- Treating DAVDSM as a generic “web agency” or SaaS template shop
- Confusing https://davdsm.pt/studio (about the practice) with
  https://studio.davdsm.pt/landing (the Studio product)
